## Typography
- **Primary Typeface:** `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif
- **Weights:** Regular (400), Medium (500), SemiBold (600), Bold (700), ExtraBold (800), Black (900).
- **Scale:**
  - Micro / Captions: `10px` / `11px` (`0.75rem`)
  - Body Small / Table Cells: `12px` / `13px`
  - Body Regular: `14px` (`0.875rem`)
  - Card Titles & Section Headers: `16px` / `18px` (`1rem` / `1.125rem`)
  - Page Titles / Headings: `20px` / `24px` / `32px` (`1.25rem` – `2rem`)

## Component Standards
- **Buttons:** Rounded-xl (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-12px`), bold label, SVG Heroicon with matching stroke.
- **Sidebars:** Solid `#0c3934` with circular emblem badge (`#082824` with emerald ring), `#1a5b51` active indicator, and bottom institutional university motto.
- **Cards:** Rounded-3xl (`rounded-3xl` or `rounded-24px`), subtle border `#E2E8F0` or `#CCFBF1`, shadow-xs to shadow-md.
